01 — About

The humanization of space.

I'm Ar. Vannjon Lloyd P. Soria, a registered architect based in Metro Manila with about five years across design, BIM modelling, construction documentation, QA/QC, interiors and site management.

I've worked on both sides of the drawing — in design studios and on the construction site — so my documents are built to be understood and built, not just submitted. Today I work remotely with a BIM/CAD team across Israel and Poland, and take on freelance work for clients in Australia, New Zealand and the US.

04 — Process + AI

AI sharpens the work. Judgment signs it.

I use AI to speed up first drafts and to flag things I might miss — then I review, correct and sign off on everything myself. It supports my work; it never replaces my judgment.

CD Checker

An AI-assisted web app I built that flags possible errors and omissions in construction documents. Each flag is a prompt for me to check, not a verdict.

3D / Rendering QA Checklist

A repeatable checklist that every visualization passes before it reaches a client — a human review step, every time.

Drafting aids

Invoices, proposals and checklists I draft with AI support, then edit and finalize by hand.
